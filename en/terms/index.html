What Verbbe is

Verbbe is for organizing and playing audio files that you link from Files or that a self-hosted Verbbe server indexes on a machine you run. It is not a store, it does not license songs, and it does not operate a commercial streaming catalog. It does not connect to Apple Music, YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, or similar services to download or stream their catalogs.

Your files and rights

You are responsible for having the right to use the files you play, both on the iPhone and on the server. Verbbe does not review or clear the content of your library. If you do not have those rights, do not use the app to play them.

Optional server

The server is software you install (for example with Docker) on your PC or network. You choose the URL, accounts, and music folders. The developer does not host that server. You are responsible for its security, who you give access to, and that it only indexes files you may use.

You can keep it on your Wi-Fi only, or publish it as an HTTPS link (Tailscale Funnel on the computer). That link is public; anyone who has it reaches the sign-in screen. The phone only needs Verbbe. You are responsible for passwords and who you share it with.

Remote playback streams from your machine to your iPhone. It is not a developer-operated streaming service and not access to third-party catalogs.

Accounts

No account is required for the local library. If you create the server admin or sign in, that account lives on your server. The administrator can delete other users in the app. Signing out on the iPhone does not delete music on the server.

Verbena

Verbena is optional: it connects iPhones on the same local network so you can listen together. The host shares library metadata and playback control with anyone who has the code. Audio files are not copied to the guest. You are responsible for who you share the code with and for having the right to play those files in that session. It is not a public streaming service and not operated by the developer.

How it works

Verbbe doesn’t guarantee that every file, tag, cover, or lyric will be read correctly. The result depends on how your files are tagged and on whether the iPhone can reach the server.

If you unlink or empty a linked folder, that folder’s songs disappear from the local library.
